How to read this

The useful comparison is not just how much goes into super. It is the reduction in take-home pay, the tax saved, and the possible future balance. The planner assumes extra contributions are salary-sacrificed concessional contributions and deducts 15% contributions tax.

The $30,000 concessional cap includes employer and salary-sacrifice contributions across all funds. This estimate does not include other contributions, carry-forward cap amounts, HELP/TSL, Medicare levy surcharge, insurance or fund fees. Division 293 may add another 15% tax for higher-income earners. This is a guide, not financial advice.
