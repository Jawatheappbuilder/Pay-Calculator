How to read this

The useful comparison is not just how much goes into super. It is the reduction in take-home pay, the tax saved, and the possible future balance. The planner assumes extra contributions are salary-sacrificed concessional contributions and deducts 15% contributions tax.

The $30,000 concessional cap includes employer and salary-sacrifice contributions across all funds. This estimate does not include other contributions, carry-forward cap amounts, HELP/TSL, Medicare levy surcharge, insurance or fund fees. Division 293 may add another 15% tax for higher-income earners. This is a guide, not financial advice.

Compare purchased leave

Each purchased week costs one week of the selected classification's base rate, spread evenly over 26 fortnightly pays.

The workdays setting only converts purchased weeks into extra days off. It does not change the salary calculation.

Assumptions

Purchased leave is treated as a pre-tax reduction equal to the selected weekly base rate multiplied by the weeks purchased. Employer super remains calculated on the full base-rate earnings. Overtime, allowances and the other settings currently entered in the pay calculator remain included.

Actual payroll treatment can depend on your employer's purchased-leave policy. Use this as an estimate and confirm the arrangement before applying.
